# PageHearth branding policy — APPROVED

## Ownership and software rights

PixelFF is the developer's public brand, not a separate legal entity. The
individual developer personally retains copyright in original PageHearth source
and uses “Copyright © 2026 PixelFF” as public attribution. This policy does not
transfer ownership or assert registered trademark status.

PageHearth and PixelFF names, logos and original brand artwork are separate from
MPL-2.0 application code. Nothing here restricts MPL rights to use, modify or
redistribute covered software, including commercially, or to preserve required
notices. Original UI code/CSS remains covered code, not reserved branding merely
because it is visible. Third-party resources retain their own rights and terms.

## Official branding and unofficial forks

Recipients may reproduce the unchanged official PageHearth mark and wordmark
with authentic, unchanged official releases. This permission concerns branding
only; it does not restrict redistribution or modification of MPL-covered code.
It does not grant permission to present a modified fork as an official release
or use official branding as that fork's own product identity.

Truthful identification, screenshots, reviews and independently authored guides
or documentation of PageHearth and unofficial forks are permitted, including
incidental reproduction of PageHearth branding in those screenshots and uses
such as “based on PageHearth.” These uses must not misleadingly imply official
status, sponsorship or endorsement by the developer or PixelFF.

Other uses of original brand artwork require the developer's permission unless
already permitted by its applicable license or by law. PixelFF's separate
red-panda artwork is not included in the express PageHearth artwork permission.
Nothing here limits lawful attribution, fair use, fair dealing or other rights
that exist independently of this policy.

## Documentation and third-party material

Independently authored guides, reviews and screenshots do not require permission
merely because they discuss PageHearth or an unofficial fork. Copying
PageHearth-owned guide text, supplied illustrations or standalone artwork beyond
the permissions above remains subject to its applicable license, separate
permission from the developer, or rights provided by law.

The PageHearth book-and-flame mark is manually constructed vector geometry. The
wordmark uses Georgia typography without distributing Georgia font files. This
policy grants no rights in third-party fonts or other third-party material and
does not claim ownership of them. Historical artwork is not automatically
covered by permission for the current official mark and wordmark.

## Curated source archive

Our curated source archive omits artwork, guide content, historical reports and
Git history while retaining application/build/test source and notices. This is
a decision about what we include in that archive, not a restriction on anyone's
MPL rights, including redistribution of MPL-covered material from Git history.
Other material retains its applicable rights and permissions. Building with
excluded branding assets requires separately available authorized assets or
local replacements; this does not add conditions to the source-code license.
